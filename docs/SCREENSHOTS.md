## Capture and privacy record

- viewport: `1280 × 720` for every image;
- format: real PNG bytes, not a renamed browser encoding;
- data: public official-source metadata plus fictional brands, creators, products, cases, and events;
- approval state at capture: nine requirement candidates, zero requirement reviews, zero mappings, and zero findings;
- privacy QA: visual inspection plus OCR scan for personal paths, emails, secrets, sessions, private-project names, and real-person or real-merchant indicators;
- external effects: none; screenshot capture used read-only navigation against the local UI.

Screenshots are documentation artifacts. Reproducible assertions remain in automated tests and the evidence-only [`make demo-rehearsal`](DEMO_GUIDE.md) preflight.
